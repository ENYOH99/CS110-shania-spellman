1. **Start** - the program begins.
2. **Read number 8** - the user enters a number.
3. **8 % 2 == 0?** - the % operator finds the remainder of a division, and since even numbers divide evenly by 2, their remainder will always be 0.
   - **Yes** -> print "Even".
   - **No** -> print "Odd".
4. **End** - the program stops.
